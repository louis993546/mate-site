Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Developer: Tsz Ho Tsai (Louis)

1. Summary

Mate is a media player for Android developed by Tsz Ho Tsai (Louis). Mate does not require an account and does not contain ads. Mate does not send your personal data to servers operated by the developer. We do not sell your personal data.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Information needed for the app to function—such as your media server connections, library metadata, playback progress, favorites, and app settings—is stored locally in app storage on your device. Server passwords are protected using Android system encryption. This information is not sent to the developer.

3. Local Network & Device Media Access

Mate accesses your local network to discover and connect to media servers and playback receivers (such as Google Cast devices) that you choose. If you grant access to videos on your device or select files through the system file picker, Mate reads those files locally for browsing and playback. Mate does not send your media files to the developer; if you choose to cast a local video, the video is streamed to the selected receiver on your local network for playback.

4. Third-Party Services

Mate does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Optional casting features and app distribution rely on Google Play Services and Google Play, which may collect crash, diagnostic, and service data under Google's Privacy Policy. Through Google Play Console, the developer may receive aggregated crash and performance reports derived from that diagnostic data.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

To remove Mate's local data from this device, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall Mate (you can also delete saved servers or clear cache inside the app). This removes data stored locally by Mate; it does not remove media files or logs on external media servers you connected to, or device backups managed by your operating system.

6. Children's Privacy

Mate is a general-audience media player and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region).

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Mate evolves. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
